Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Monkey app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Eugene Belenkov (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. The Application is supported by advertising. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location. The Application and the third-party services it uses automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) – a unique, user-resettable identifier provided by your device, used to show ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, measure ad performance, find out which advertising campaign an installation of the Application came from, prevent fraud and, unless you opt out, personalize ads.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region), used by the advertising and analytics services to select relevant ads and to prevent fraud. The Application does not request access to your device's location.
  • Device information – device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type.
  • Usage data – session duration, in-app actions (for example, games played, purchases made with in-game coins, ads watched), interactions with ads.
  • Crash reports and performance data.

This information is used to show ads, to reward you with in-game coins for watching ads, to count installations of the Application and find out which advertising campaigns they came from, to analyze how the Application is used, to fix errors and to improve the Application. The Service Provider does not sell your information.


Advertising

The Application shows ads, including rewarded video ads and interstitial ads, through the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads). Yandex may deliver ads from partner networks through mediation, including Unity Ads. These networks receive the information described above directly from your device and process it under their own privacy policies.

Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. You can opt out of personalized ads or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). After that you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided information to the Application, please contact the Service Provider at evgenybelenkov344@gmail.com so that the necessary actions can be taken.


Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider keeps analytics data only for as long as necessary to analyze and improve the Application. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own privacy policies.

Your in-game progress and coins are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Application or clear its data.

To request deletion of data associated with your device, contact the Service Provider at evgenybelenkov344@gmail.com. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings, which disconnects previously collected advertising data from your device.


Your Choices
  • Opt out of personalized ads or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings, as described above.
  • Stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall processes available on your device or through Google Play.

Security

The Service Provider is committed to protecting user privacy. All data transmitted by the Application to third-party services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
